# Live session

- `LiveService` keeps the connected LIVE in memory for Home (`LiveSession`): when it started, its totals, and its last `ACTIVITY_LIMIT` events. `registerConnectorEvents` starts it on `CONNECTED` and ends it on `DISCONNECTED`.
- Every `CONNECTED` reply and push comes from `connectionState` (`src/main/helper/events.ts`), with `platforms`, how each platform that's on is doing (`ConnectorService.status()`, `.claude/rules/platforms.md`). Its `live` is true once the session has started (`startedAt !== null`), so a reconnect stays live and a connect in progress isn't. Home and the title bar's tally show LIVE from it, and Home keeps its spinner while `success` is true without `live` or `waiting`. `success` (connecting, connected, reconnecting or looking for the LIVE) is the profile lock.
  - `reconnecting`: the connection dropped and is coming back, and the session goes on. Home's status line says Reconnecting… in place of the timer (the user's call).
  - `waiting`: the connector looks for a LIVE that stopped on its own (`.claude/rules/connector.md`), with no session. Home says Waiting for the LIVE…, and its Disconnect ends the look.
- With several platforms the session is the first one up's to the last one down. Home's tab for each platform shows its state, and its own Connect (a `CONNECT` with its platform) or Disconnect (a `DISCONNECT` with its platform, the others going on). The title bar's tally and the profile lock follow the session.
- Layout asks `IS_CONNECTED` itself, next to `GET_SETTINGS`, since main pushes `CONNECTED` on its own only as the connection changes (a stop, a drop, a reconnect, the look for the LIVE ending). A window reloaded mid-LIVE (`reloadWhenGone`) then knows on any page, for the tally, the profile lock and Update's confirm.
- The gift and coin totals count gifts as they come, through `GiftStreaks` (see `.claude/rules/alerts.md`), reset when a LIVE starts. The feed lists a combo once, on `repeatEnd`, like gift alerts, with the streak's total.
- A chat item carries the translation its event came with: Chat translation translates before any listener gets the chat (`.claude/rules/translation.md`), so items still never change once recorded, as `ActivitySection`'s memo expects.
- Joins in a row share one item, each handle once (`_joined`), up to `JOIN_LIMIT` viewers, then a new item starts. Likes and room updates stay out of the feed; the stats show them.
- The likes stat is the highest `totalLikes` seen (`registerConnectorEvents`), since TikTok sometimes lowers its total for 30–50 s. It goes back to 0 with the other stats on `DISCONNECTED`.
- `viewers` holds each minute's highest viewer count from `ROOM_USER` since `startedAt`, index = whole minutes since it (`recordViewers`). A minute without a reading repeats the last count, a clock set back stays in the minute it reached, and it stops at `VIEWER_MINUTES` (24 h).
- `platforms` keeps each platform's own totals and minutes, for Home's tabs: a viewer's `platform` picks the totals (missing is TikTok), and each `ROOM_USER` records `ConnectorService.viewers()` too. A platform's minutes start at the session's first, 0 before its first count and after it left, so the tooltip's times stay right. `VIEWS` and `CONNECTED`'s `stats` carry each platform's viewers now (`platformViewers`).
  - Minute 0's counts push nothing. The first count of each later minute pushes the session once, through its throttle, and `DISCONNECTED` cancels a push still waiting.
  - Home draws every minute but the last, then the live `VIEWS` count, so the chart's end moves with the readout.
- The feed and the viewers stay after the LIVE ends, until the next one starts.
- Home shows the chart, the numbers and the top gifters only while LIVE (the user's call), each only with a platform that has it (`liveStats` in `pages/Home/helper.ts`). Offline it's the platforms' tabs and the feature switches, beside the feed.
- The numbers follow Home's tabs (`.claude/rules/platforms.md`): a platform's tab shows its own while it's in the LIVE (`livePlatforms`: up now, or with numbers in it, so one that left keeps them), each tab with its viewers now. With two or more in the LIVE, All comes after them and adds them up (the user's calls). Home picks the first platform's tab until another is picked. `liveView` picks a tab's viewers, minutes, stats and top gifters.
- A platform's tab tops who sent the most in its currency, named by it: Top gifters (TikTok's coins), Top Bits, Top Kicks, Top Super Chats, each only with an amount. Twitch's, YouTube's and Kick's tabs also top who gifted the most subs, apart (Top gifted subs, `subsByPlatform`), the user's call: one list mixing Bits and subs read as gifts. None on All.
- Home also asks `GET_LIVE_GIFTERS` (this LIVE's top gifters on each platform, `byPlatform`, and its top sub gifters, `subsByPlatform`, both empty outside one) and `GET_SONGS` (the queue as overlays get it), and `init.ts` pushes `LIVE_GIFTERS` with the overlays' `tokio:gifters` (see `.claude/rules/history.md`) and `SONGS` on every `MUSIC_CHANGE`, unthrottled. Now playing is the queue's playing song, while Music is connected.
- The stat tiles follow the tab's platforms (`liveStats`; All's are the LIVE's): likes, total views and shares only with TikTok (Twitch has no total views and sends 0), New followers only with a platform that sends follows, gifts with one that sends gifts (not Kick), Gifted subs with one that sends them (Twitch, YouTube, Kick), then one tile per currency, never added up (the user's call): Coins with TikTok, Kicks with Kick (from `KICKS`), Bits with Twitch and Super Chats in US dollars with YouTube. Top gifters show on each platform's tab in its currency (`PLATFORM_CURRENCY`, `amountLabel`), with the subs they gifted (`sentLabel`: only the subs when they spent nothing). They never show on All, which would rank one currency against another. A feed gift row shows its price in the sender's currency, and nothing for YouTube's Gifts (the user's call: hide the zero). Past six tiles, `LiveSection` splits them over two rows. Feed rows show the platform's icon once the profile uses two or more, and the feed's kinds are the ones its platforms send (`feedKinds`). Twitch adds the kinds `sub`, `giftsub`, `cheer`, `raid` and `redeem`, YouTube `superchat` (its memberships and gifted memberships use `sub` and `giftsub`), and Kick `kicks`, with the message pinned with them, as a cheer's. Bits, Kicks, Super Chats and gifted subs count in the totals even while the feed hides them.
- `settings.activityFeed` picks the kinds of events the feed keeps, for every profile, saved from Home's `FeedModal` with `SET_ACTIVITY_FEED`. `LiveService.setFeed` applies it when the app loads and on save, dropping the items it now hides.
- Hidden events aren't kept but still count toward the totals.
- Past LIVEs are kept by `HistoryService`, which counts the same span on its own: see `.claude/rules/history.md`.

## Super Chats in US dollars

- `LiveService` keeps each Super Chat's amount by currency (`parseAmount`, `src/main/helper/amount.ts`, which reads YouTube's en-US text with a symbol map built from `Intl`), and `getSession()` adds them up in US dollars through `RatesService.toUsd` (the user's calls: always USD, no setting). So a Super Chat that came before the rates loaded isn't lost: `usd` is null until they do, and Home's tile shows a dash (`NO_MONEY`). Code that only asks whether it's LIVE, like the uptime tick, reads the `startedAt` getter instead of that copy.
- `RatesService` (`src/main/services/rates`) reads Frankfurter's US dollar rates (`api.frankfurter.dev`, free, no key, listed in the notices), keeps them in memory for a day, sends one request at a time and backs off from a minute to an hour after a failure, with a warning and no notice. Dollars need no rates, so a LIVE without other currencies never fetches. A currency the rates lack is left out of the total, warned once.
- **Each Super Chat's own dollars:**
  - `ConnectorService` puts `usd` on each `SUPER_CHAT` as it forwards it (`setRates`, `RatesService.usdOf`), so alerts, Studio, games, history and overlays all get the same value.
  - It's null when the amount can't be read, before the rates load, or for a currency they lack, never a made-up 0.
  - History waits for the rates on its own (`.claude/rules/history.md`), and a Super Chat row's minimum lets one without dollars through (`.claude/rules/alerts.md`).
- Rates load on `CONNECTED` when YouTube is on and when it comes up later (the existing `onPlatform` callback, which holds one listener), and their arrival pushes the session and Studio's numbers again.
